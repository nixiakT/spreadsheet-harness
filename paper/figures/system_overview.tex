\caption{\method's two terminal paths. Every arm uses the same observer
instrumentation to snapshot each submit before the gate, including rejected,
invalid, and unchanged attempts. Acceptance binds the
exact attempt/response/call, then records candidate $\rightarrow$
postprocess/recalculation $\rightarrow$ final $\rightarrow$ consistency
certificate $\rightarrow$ byte-identical scoring replica. Finalization checks
harness-observed transitions but neither reruns the model nor replays every
model-produced witness. Without an accepted candidate, an audited-noncompletion
row receives only an observer-generated finalization integrity record; its
reason and attempt ledger are auditable, but no accepted deliverable is claimed.
Frozen attempts are scored only after termination and never fed back. Double
outlines denote immutable or content-bound records. The certificate is not a
digital signature or hostile-host security mechanism.}
