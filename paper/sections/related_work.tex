\section{Setting and related work}

\paragraph{Spreadsheet agents and benchmarks.}
SpreadsheetBench evaluates real-world spreadsheet manipulation with input and
oracle workbooks \citep{ma2024spreadsheetbench}. SpreadsheetAgent improves
large-workbook understanding through an extracted structural sketch, localized
code/image/\LaTeX{} views, and multi-agent verification
\citep{ren2026spreadsheetagent}. Spreadsheet-RL combines isolated Excel
workspaces, native tools, an inspect--modify--verify harness, and outcome-based
reinforcement learning \citep{chi2026spreadsheetrl}. SpreadsheetBench~2 shifts
evaluation to 321 business workflows with an average of 11.8 sheets and 593.5
modified cells; its 100 debugging tasks hide both error type and location
\citep{zhu2026spreadsheetbench2}. We reuse localized representations and native
tools, but study when a verification observation is sufficient to authorize
completion.

\paragraph{Spreadsheet correctness and provenance.}
Spreadsheet Guardian separates spreadsheet test rules from their execution and
reruns them during maintenance \citep{kulesz2018spreadsheetguardian}; automated
spreadsheet provenance also predates our work
\citep{asuncion2013spreadsheetprovenance}. We claim neither mechanism first.
Instead, computed effects trigger evidence bound to affected scope, exact
revision, postprocessing, and evaluator bytes.

\paragraph{Experience-derived skills.}
Trace2Skill analyzes successful and failed trajectories in parallel, proposes
trajectory-local patches, and hierarchically consolidates them into a portable
skill directory \citep{ni2026trace2skill}. We evaluate this as a
procedure-only inspired baseline; \method's runtime contract and artifact lineage do not
depend on how a procedure was authored. Joint procedure/contract evolution is
kept outside the primary claims unless a same-pool factorial and cross-model
transfer study are complete. Agent Workflow Memory also induces reusable
workflows from trajectories \citep{wang2025agentworkflowmemory}; neither form of
procedural memory itself enforces acceptance.

\paragraph{Runtime enforcement and trace conformance.}
Runtime verification has long separated execution from trace-property monitors
\citep{leucker2009runtimeverification}. AgentSpec provides customizable
trigger/check/enforce rules \citep{wang2026agentspec}; VIGIL and AgentLTL add
finite-trace, artifact/value-flow, and typed procedural constraints
\citep{li2026vigil,elkoussy2026agentltl}, while guardrails separate policy from
generation \citep{rebedea2023nemo}. Thus temporal enforcement and policy
compilation are not our novelty. Our spreadsheet front end reveals computed
Python effects, recalculation changes, and stale render identities absent from a
raw call trace. We compare the same policy engine on raw and enriched events,
not against a deliberately weak adapter.

\paragraph{Artifact lineage.}
Software supply-chain systems such as in-toto bind declared steps to materials
and products using authenticated metadata \citep{torresarias2019intoto}. Our
accepted-deliverable certificate instead hash-binds candidate evidence,
recalculation, final checks, and scoring replica under a trusted host and
storage; the
all-row observer record provides only lineage consistency. Neither provides
signatures, external transparency, or hostile-host authentication.

\paragraph{Scope of the guarantee.}
Process conformance and task correctness are complementary. A compliant agent
may confidently modify the wrong target, while a noncompliant agent may pass by
chance. We therefore use the official workbook evaluator as the primary outcome
and evidence compliance as a separately audited mechanism. Our conditional
invariant covers trusted witnesses for configured obligations; it does not
prove that those obligations fully encode user intent.
