\section{Revision-aware spreadsheet evidence}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Artifacts, effects, and scopes}

Workbook artifact $\artifact_v=(v,h_v)$ pairs a monotonic publication index
with the SHA-256 of its exact bytes. Every byte-changing publication increments
$v$, including recalculation, recovery, and semantic no-ops; only a committed
task mutation sets the separate user-edit flag. Reads, renders, and image views
publish no artifact. A mutation first writes an isolated staging copy, from
which the harness computes

\begin{equation}
  \effects_r = \operatorname{Diff}(W_r,W_{r+1})
  = (\effects^{\mathrm{value}},\effects^{\mathrm{formula}},
     \effects^{\mathrm{style}},\effects^{\mathrm{structure}},
     \effects^{\mathrm{visual}},\effects^{\mathrm{unknown}}).
\end{equation}

Components contain workbook, sheet, or finite rectangular scopes; visual events
also bind a render manifest and page. The XLSX diff covers values, formulas,
styles, sheet/row/column structure, merges, tables, layout, names, and supported
charts. Unexplained changed ZIP parts or ambiguous objects fail closed as
\texttt{unknown}. A byte-only semantic no-op still advances $v$ without setting
the user-edit flag. Native-tool reports are not the effect oracle: comparing
pre/post staging bytes gives direct openpyxl or other opaque Python edits the
same observation path.

\subsection{Prospective target grounding}

Post-edit checks can faithfully verify the wrong target. Before a protected
mutation, the model therefore declares finite $\widehat{\scope}$ and cites
earlier harness observations $I$ on the exact source artifact, requiring

\begin{equation}
  \bigcup_{i\in I}\operatorname{scope}(i)\supseteq\widehat{\scope}
  \quad\land\quad
  \forall i\in I:\operatorname{artifact}(i)=\artifact_v.
\end{equation}

After staging, publication additionally requires
$\operatorname{scope}(\effects_v)\subseteq\widehat{\scope}$ for the complete
native or opaque-code footprint. Unknown effects, stale citations, uninspected
expansion, or declaration reuse reject it. This proves observation and
containment, not that $\widehat{\scope}$ matches user intent; gold masks remain
evaluation-only.

\subsection{Contracts and evidence obligations}

A contract is a versioned trigger/sequence list in a closed YAML grammar over
harness-defined effects, events, scope transforms, artifact selectors, and
predicates---never Python or a model-evaluated condition. A match creates

\begin{equation}
  o=(m,\scope_o,(g_1,\ldots,g_K),j,Q_j),
\end{equation}

where $m$ and $\scope_o$ are the trigger and derived scope;
$g_j=(e_j,a_j,\rho_j,\pi_j)$ specifies event, dynamic artifact, scope relation,
and predicate; and $Q_j$ is same-stage evidence. Advancement requires

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:witness}
  \forall q\in Q_j:\;
  \operatorname{type}(q)=e_j \land
  \operatorname{artifact}(q)=a_j \land
  \pi_j(q) \land \operatorname{after}(q,m),
  \qquad
  \bigcup_{q\in Q_j}\operatorname{scope}(q)\;\rho_j\;\scope_o.
\end{equation}

Disjoint reads accumulate only within one stage and artifact; a byte publication
clears that coverage. Dynamic selectors make transitions explicit: a formula mutation
$\artifact_v\!\rightarrow\!\artifact_{v+1}$ requires
$\operatorname{recalc}(\text{source}=\artifact_{v+1},
\text{output}=\artifact_{v+2})$, after which readback binds dynamically to
$\artifact_{v+2}$. Visual rules bind the current artifact, render manifest, and
viewed page.

A transactional failure that leaves the source unchanged creates no blocker;
partial or uncertain recovery creates one cleared only by a later computed
footprint covering its scope. This does not prove shared intent.

Online \emph{shadow} and \emph{enforce} compute identical diagnostics; only
enforce blocks submission. Because visible diagnostics can alter trajectories,
offline replay of immutable no-monitor traces estimates detection, not recovery.
Budgets remain fixed. Common observer instrumentation snapshots every submit
before any gate, and post-termination attempt scores never return to the model.

\subsection{Postprocessing and finalization records}

Submission is only a finalization intent: evaluator recalculation may mutate
cached values after the last model check. \method therefore records

\begin{equation}
  \artifact_{\mathrm{candidate}} \xrightarrow{\text{postprocess}}
  \artifact_{\mathrm{final}},\qquad
  \operatorname{ScorerCopy}(\artifact_{\mathrm{final}},p_{\mathrm{score}}).
\end{equation}

An \texttt{accepted\_candidate} binds its exact terminal response and call to a
pre-gate attempt and replayable candidate evidence. Every other termination is
\texttt{audited\_noncompletion}: its observer-only integrity record binds the
terminal reason, attempts, trajectory, candidate, and later lineage, but has
null accepted evidence and claims no accepted submission, deliverable, or
correctness.

Postprocessing emits a ledger footprint. Accepted candidates require a complete,
non-semantic transition; semantic or unknown effects fail closed. Noncompletion
may record a complete or fail-closed unknown transition because it transfers no
candidate evidence. Both branches record reopened formula/cached-value views
and a workbook-scope final scan. Accepted visual evidence transfers only when a
fresh render matches sheet/page and page bytes. The scan is not a model
inspection. The accepted branch binds a read-only scoring replica; noncompletion
binds only a diagnostic copy, never a deliverable.

The canonical observer record contains outcome, optional accepted evidence,
initial/candidate/final artifacts, transition/event-chain digests,
recalculation metadata, final scan, and copy digest. Only its accepted branch
is an accepted-deliverable certificate and calls the copy a scoring replica;
local paths are excluded.

The scorer-facing copy is created atomically inside the isolated task
directory. For an accepted candidate, the harness checks

\begin{equation}
  h(W_{\mathrm{final}})=h(W_{\mathrm{score}})
  =h_{\mathrm{row}}^{\mathrm{output}}
  =c.\mathrm{final\_artifact.sha256}
\end{equation}

before and after scoring. The same equality and immutability checks apply to a
noncompletion's diagnostic copy without authorizing a deliverable. Fresh replay
rejects missing revisions, invalid witnesses, digest or score-time mutation,
and contract/output disagreement.

The finalization record provides content consistency only under a trusted host,
controller, and storage; the accepted-deliverable certificate is not a digital
signature. The trusted computing base includes diff and event instrumentation,
monitor/controller memory, result storage, recalculation backend, scorer, and
auditor. Model text, tool self-reports, and candidate bytes are untrusted. The
scheme does not prevent a compromised or coordinating host from consistently
rewriting the artifacts, ledger, record, and timestamps. Preventing that attack
requires an external append-only anchor or transparency log, authenticated
signatures, and a trusted clock. SHA-256 collision, full user intent, and
Excel--LibreOffice equivalence are also outside the guarantee.

\paragraph{Conditional evidence invariant.}
Under that trusted base, every accepted candidate has a post-trigger,
harness-emitted witness sequence on the selected artifact whose same-stage scope
union and predicates satisfy each obligation. Each protected mutation also has
a same-source declaration containing its staged footprint. Induction follows
because only Equation~\ref{eq:witness} advances a stage and publication
invalidates mismatched progress. An accepted deliverable additionally has a
complete non-semantic transition, final scan, required pixel equivalence, and a
byte-identical scoring replica. A noncompletion has only an observer integrity
record binding artifact-to-diagnostic-copy lineage. Appendix~\ref{app:invariant}
gives cases; neither invariant proves correctness or cryptographic security.

\section{Contract provenance and procedure baselines}
\label{sec:contract-provenance}

The core uses one preregistered human-authored contract, fixed across shadow and
enforce. Trace2Skill-inspired prose is a procedure-only whole-system baseline.
Joint procedure/contract evolution remains outside the claims unless a separate
same-pool factorial and cross-model transfer study is completed.
