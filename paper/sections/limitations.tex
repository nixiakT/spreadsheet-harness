\section{Limitations and conclusion}

\method{} checks evidence about configured effects, not the complete meaning of
an instruction. Containment cannot ensure that a model chose the intended
declaration. The finite contract is not full workbook correctness: compliance
may accompany a wrong answer, while fail-closed handling may reject a correct
but unsupported workbook.

The trusted diff, adapters, storage, predicates, recalculation, scorer guard,
hashing, and auditor can misread macros, external links, arrays, embedded
objects, or vendor charts. Our independent OOXML oracle reduces shared-parser
risk but mostly uses one producer and is not an Excel specification.
LibreOffice/Excel disagreement is therefore reported on a frozen subset;
unknown effects fail closed.

An immutable attempt is an observation, not a correct solution; post-termination
scores cannot identify recovery or a later gate effect. An audited
noncompletion has no accepted deliverable: its bundle, certificate-shaped
fields, final artifact, and diagnostic copy are only an observer-generated
lineage-integrity record. Finalization checks observed transitions and a fresh
scan, but neither reruns the model nor reconstructs every model witness on final
bytes; only explicit compatibility rules transfer evidence.

Content consistency assumes a trusted host and storage. Coordinating components
can rewrite artifacts, records, and timestamps consistently; prevention needs
an external append-only anchor or transparency log, authenticated signatures,
and a trusted clock. Hashes alone do not authenticate a hostile scorer or OS.
Two model families, three seeds, small Visualization-24/Debugging-100 suites,
and service drift limit generalization. Trace2Skill-inspired procedures are
whole-system baselines; joint distillation remains an optional controlled study.

We therefore pair official correctness with false-completion, coverage,
rejection/recovery, collateral damage, cost, and backend disagreement.
\method{} makes ``done'' auditable only for specified checks and lineage; it is
neither a correctness proof nor a cryptographic guarantee.
