\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

We ask whether (Q1) semantic event lifting separates valid and invalid
executions that are provably indistinguishable from their raw traces; (Q2)
online enforcement reduces unsafe accepted delivery without unacceptable
Accuracy or terminal-coverage loss; (Q3) grounding and finalization lineage
address off-target edits and postprocessing drift; and (Q4) what rejection,
recovery, collateral damage, latency, token, and trusted-component costs result.

\subsection{Benchmarks and protocol}

\paragraph{SpreadsheetBench-Verified.}
Trace2Skill uses a nominal 400-example verified collection, with indices
0:200 for evolution and 200:400 for held-out evaluation
\citep{ma2024spreadsheetbench,ni2026trace2skill}. After the documented local
exclusions, our pinned copy contains 198 usable held-out examples; we never call
all 400 held out. A one-shot study on the final 79 locally unexposed tasks
concerns a predecessor harness designed before SheetLedger. An inadvertent
read-only interim audit makes that study exploratory deployment context only;
it supports neither confirmatory inference nor evidence for the new method.
The original benchmark's sibling-
replay Soft/Hard protocol (\texttt{solution\_once\_apply\_n}) and our one-agent-
per-workbook protocol (\texttt{agent\_per\_workbook}) have different units and
are never pooled or presented as the same leaderboard setting.

\paragraph{SpreadsheetBench~2.}
The external study seals all 100 debugging instance files and identifiers
before development \citep{zhu2026spreadsheetbench2}. At least three task
descriptions are public, so a frozen metadata-only matcher partitions a
publicly exposed stratum before workbook access. The primary confirmatory
analysis uses the remaining artifact-unseen stratum; all 100 tasks form a
preregistered sensitivity analysis, with exposed results shown separately.
Artifact-unseen describes this project's access, not model-training freshness.
We run Visualization-24 only as an exploratory visual study until typed object
grounding is implemented, report official Modification and Accuracy, and never
use these instances for artifact selection.

\paragraph{Evidence-oracle suite.}
Randomized workbooks cross value, formula, style, structure, visual, opaque-code,
rollback, and lineage effects with valid traces and minimal stale, wrong-scope,
postprocess, and field-tamper attacks. A standard-library ZIP/XML oracle shares
no parser with the runtime comparator and covers shared/array formulas, tables,
names, charts, lossy extensions, and opaque parts. Held-out handcrafted cases
and valid controls measure both missed attacks and over-rejection; the sealed
tamper corpus is not used to author the contract.

\paragraph{Models and backends.}
The causal matrix uses pinned Qwen3.5-35B-A3B plus a different model family,
with seeds 41/42/43. We record availability of both Trace2Skill checkpoints;
reproducibly available Qwen3.5-122B-A10B receives a frozen, separately labeled
protocol-alignment extension, not a replacement for the cross-family run.
Opaque service aliases are labeled non-reproducible. LibreOffice is primary; a
frozen Excel-COM subset measures formula, render, and official-score disagreement.

Every comparison is paired by task and fixes decoding, initial files, backend,
and arm-level call/token/time ceilings. A seeded Latin square balances order;
manifests record code, prompts, model/service revision, and infrastructure
incidence. There is no outcome-dependent retry, and errors stay in the Accuracy
denominator. In every arm, common observer instrumentation freezes each submit
pre-gate as an immutable attempt; attempts are scored only after model
termination and never fed back. Termination without an accepted candidate
remains an audited noncompletion, not a missing row.

\subsection{Baselines and controlled variants}

B0 is code-only. B1 is the Spreadsheet-RL-inspired native-tool adapter (no
RL-trained checkpoint). B2 is the SpreadsheetAgent-inspired multi-format
adapter, adding structural, range, text, and rendered views. Trace2Skill is a
procedure-only inspired whole-system baseline over B2. These three prior-work-
derived rows are clean-room inspired adapters and are never called official
reproductions unless the released implementation and its full protocol are
actually used. The \method{} contract is researcher-authored and fixed across
B5, B6, and FULL.

One B6 online execution durably forks B5 at its first gate-eligible submit,
before feedback; B5 is neither a second sample nor reconstructed from B6's
later outcome. B6 may recover after rejection, while FULL shares B6's terminal
and factual finalization assessment and adds only lineage authorization. The
family publishes atomically or not at all. FULL, B5, and B6 hold G2 fixed. G0
omits declarations, G1 elicits them without containment, and G2 enforces
containment, so G1--G0 and G2--G1 isolate the two increments.

A core, same-policy comparison crosses raw versus enriched events within a
representative generic engine; the containing $2\times2$ also crosses
generic/restricted policy, with policies and visible fields published. Oracle
ablations remove one of revision/scope binding, semantic or opaque-code diffing,
stale invalidation, recalculation lineage, or recovery linkage. Joint
procedure/contract distillation is optional secondary work after the fixed matrix
is frozen.

\subsection{Metrics and statistics}

The headline requires two gates: same-policy enriched versus raw events on
invalid-attack acceptance (valid-control-rejection guardrail), and B6 versus its
pre-feedback B5 fork on unsafe accepted delivery (Accuracy and terminal-
coverage guardrails). Failure of either blocks the claim. FULL--B6, G1--G0, and
G2--G1 are secondary paired mechanism contrasts.

An \emph{unsafe accepted delivery} is a selected task whose path authorizes
delivery but the official evaluator fails or required lineage is invalid; its
rate over all tasks is the enforcement estimand. We also report attempts,
coverage, rejection/recovery, audited noncompletion, target precision/recall,
collateral effects, and conditional risk so abstention remains visible. Against
scorer-only reference masks, declaration and actual-effect precision/recall plus
area ratios expose the vacuous ``inspect and declare everything'' strategy.

Costs cover tokens, calls, wall time, component CPU, transitions, and backend
disagreement. Three-seed intervals resample task clusters while preserving each
arm-by-seed vector. The headline uses an intersection--union decision: both
principal gates must pass their frozen level-$0.05$ paired test and guardrail;
secondary contrasts receive no confirmatory multiplicity claim. Model families
are not pooled. Margins and procedures are frozen after design-stage precision
analysis; Appendix~\ref{app:statistics} gives details.

\subsection{Mechanism, robustness, and cost analyses}

The oracle reports attack acceptance and valid-control rejection for all
ablations and event/policy cells, plus a paired B6/FULL finalization challenge
with shared candidate bytes. Real-task analyses stratify effects, recovery,
collateral damage, and certificate-consistent failures. Backend and budget
curves test recalculation assumptions and extra-call explanations; optional
distilled-artifact transfer cannot support a main claim.
