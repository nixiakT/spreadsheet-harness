\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

We ask whether (Q1) enforcing the same fixed contract used in visible shadow
mode reduces accepted-terminal false completion without unacceptable Accuracy
or accepted-terminal coverage loss;
(Q2) final-artifact lineage, target declaration elicitation, staged-containment
enforcement, and workbook-enriched events address postprocessing drift,
off-target edits, and hidden mutations;
(Q3) effects persist across two model families, three seeds, Debugging-100,
Visualization-24, and LibreOffice/Excel; and (Q4) what rejection, recovery,
collateral damage, latency, token, and trusted-component costs result.

\subsection{Benchmarks and protocol}

\paragraph{SpreadsheetBench-Verified.}
Trace2Skill uses a nominal 400-example verified collection, with indices
0:200 for evolution and 200:400 for held-out evaluation
\citep{ma2024spreadsheetbench,ni2026trace2skill}. After the documented local
exclusions, our pinned copy contains 198 usable held-out examples; we never call
all 400 held out. A one-shot study on the final 79 locally unexposed tasks
concerns a predecessor harness designed before SheetLedger. An inadvertent
read-only interim audit makes that study exploratory deployment context only;
it supports neither confirmatory inference nor evidence for the new method.
The original benchmark's sibling-
replay Soft/Hard protocol (\texttt{solution\_once\_apply\_n}) and our one-agent-
per-workbook protocol (\texttt{agent\_per\_workbook}) have different units and
are never pooled or presented as the same leaderboard setting.

\paragraph{SpreadsheetBench~2.}
The primary external study seals all 100 debugging instance files and
identifiers before development \citep{zhu2026spreadsheetbench2}. They test
unknown defects in multi-sheet workbooks and pre-edit grounding. ``Unseen''
means instance-artifact-unseen to this study: paper-level aggregates and the
published case were read, and model pretraining exposure is unknown. We check
whether that case enters the split, disclose exclusions before launch, run
Visualization-24 for visual claims, report official Modification and Accuracy,
and never use these instances for artifact selection.

\paragraph{Evidence-oracle suite.}
Randomized workbooks cross value, formula, style, structure, visual, opaque-code,
rollback, and lineage effects with valid traces and minimal stale, wrong-scope,
postprocess, and field-tamper attacks. A standard-library ZIP/XML oracle shares
no parser with the runtime comparator and covers shared/array formulas, tables,
names, charts, lossy extensions, and opaque parts. Held-out handcrafted cases
and valid controls measure both missed attacks and over-rejection; the sealed
tamper corpus is not used to author the contract.

\paragraph{Models and backends.}
The causal matrix uses pinned Qwen3.5-35B-A3B plus a different model family,
with seeds 41/42/43. We record availability of both Trace2Skill checkpoints;
reproducibly available Qwen3.5-122B-A10B receives a frozen, separately labeled
protocol-alignment extension, not a replacement for the cross-family run.
Opaque service aliases are labeled non-reproducible. LibreOffice is primary; a
frozen Excel-COM subset measures formula, render, and official-score disagreement.

Every comparison is paired by task and fixes decoding, initial files, backend,
and arm-level call/token/time ceilings. A seeded Latin square balances order;
manifests record code, prompts, model/service revision, and infrastructure
incidence. There is no outcome-dependent retry, and errors stay in the Accuracy
denominator. In every arm, common observer instrumentation freezes each submit
pre-gate as an immutable attempt; attempts are scored only after model
termination and never fed back. Termination without an accepted candidate
remains an audited noncompletion, not a missing row.

\subsection{Baselines and controlled variants}

B0 is code-only. B1 is the Spreadsheet-RL-inspired native-tool adapter (no
RL-trained checkpoint). B2 is the SpreadsheetAgent-inspired multi-format
adapter, adding structural, range, text, and rendered views. Trace2Skill is a
procedure-only inspired whole-system baseline over B2. These three prior-work-
derived rows are clean-room inspired adapters and are never called official
reproductions unless the released implementation and its full protocol are
actually used.

With one preregistered researcher-authored fixed contract, B5 is visible shadow
and B6 adds only unsatisfied-submit blocking; diagnostics, tools/model,
grounding, attempt instrumentation, and budgets stay fixed. B6 and FULL execute
equivalent deterministic postprocessing, recalculation, final-scan, and scorer-
copy operations; FULL alone adds exact accepted-terminal-to-final-to-copy
lineage enforcement. FULL includes G2 by default, and B5/B6 also keep G2 fixed.
G0 omits target declarations, G1 elicits and diagnoses declarations without
blocking staged edits, and G2 enforces staged-footprint containment. Therefore
G1--G0 estimates elicitation and G2--G1 estimates enforcement; G2--G0 is only
their total contrast, not the effect of one gate.

A core, same-policy comparison crosses raw versus enriched events within a
representative generic engine; the containing $2\times2$ also crosses
generic/restricted policy, with policies and visible fields published. Oracle
ablations remove one of revision/scope binding, semantic or opaque-code diffing,
stale invalidation, recalculation lineage, or recovery linkage. Joint
procedure/contract distillation is optional secondary work after the fixed matrix
is frozen.

\subsection{Metrics and statistics}

Primary Accuracy includes every selected task. At task level we report
first/any-attempt and accepted-terminal false completion; attempt and
accepted-terminal coverage; submission and grounding rejection/recovery; audited
noncompletion; target precision/recall; off-target and collateral effects; and
tamper acceptance/valid-control rejection. Because attempts precede the gate,
accepted-terminal false completion over all tasks is the enforcement estimand;
Accuracy, terminal coverage, and conditional risk expose abstention.

Costs include tokens, logical/HTTP/tool calls, wall time, component CPU time,
attempts, artifact transitions, and backend disagreement. For three seeds we
first average within task, then use paired task-cluster bootstrap/permutation
intervals preserving arm-by-seed vectors. Wilson/McNemar are limited to labeled
single-seed binary analyses; Modification uses paired bootstrap. Four real-task
Holm tests use the preregistered 35B checkpoint and one is model-free on the
oracle; the second family repeats real-task estimands without cross-family pooling.
Arm-masked annotation reports agreement, leakage, adjudication, role, and AI use.
Margins and clustered procedures are frozen after design-stage precision
analysis; unresolved guardrails preclude a utility-preservation claim.

\subsection{Mechanism, robustness, and cost analyses}

The oracle reports invalid-trace rejection and valid-control acceptance for all
ablations and event/policy cells. Core model-free mechanism evidence comprises
the same-policy raw/enriched comparison and a paired B6/FULL finalization
challenge in which frozen candidate bytes are shared across arms.
Real-task analyses stratify effect family,
recovery/noncompletion, collateral damage, and accepted-candidate failures
despite a consistent accepted-deliverable certificate. Visualization-24 and the
backend subset test render/recalculation
assumptions; accuracy--budget and reliability--budget curves test whether gains
come only from extra calls. The fixed contract is unchanged across models;
optional distilled-artifact transfer cannot support a main claim.
