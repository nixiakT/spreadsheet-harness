\subsection*{AI use statement}
Generative AI tools were used for synthetic-oracle data generation; conceptual
frameworks, mathematical statements, invariant arguments, and proof drafting;
hypothesis, methodology, and experiment design; method implementation;
translation between Chinese planning notes and English prose; dataset
validation, cleaning, and reformatting; qualitative failure-taxonomy analysis;
and interpretation of experimental artifacts and results. They were also used
to suggest parameters; write and review code; create artifacts and figures;
search, summarize, and format literature and references; identify gaps;
brainstorm; propose the title and paper structure; and draft and edit prose.
Models evaluated in the paper generated the spreadsheet-agent trajectories
that constitute experimental data. The authors independently checked cited
sources, reviewed generated code, data, analyses, figures, and prose, ran
deterministic tests and fresh audits, and retain responsibility for the study
design, claims, artifacts, and manuscript. No model-generated claim or citation
was accepted without verification against its source.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}
Spreadsheet automation can affect financial, operational, and scientific
records. The proposed monitor reduces one class of unsupported completion but
does not establish semantic correctness, and its accepted-deliverable
certificates must not be
presented as guarantees that a workbook is safe for consequential use. We
therefore report abstention and residual false-completion rates, document the
trusted computing base, and recommend human review for high-impact deployment.
The experiments use research datasets under their stated terms. Credentials,
private workbook contents, and identifying trajectory data are excluded from
the anonymous artifact.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
The anonymous supplement includes the harness source, restricted contract
grammar, default contracts, synthetic evidence-oracle generators, immutable
split and run manifests, environment metadata, fresh-audit logic, de-identified
task-by-arm-by-seed result rows, and scripts that regenerate every table and
figure. Appendix
Sections~\ref{app:contract}--\ref{app:statistics} specify the monitor semantics,
trusted assumptions, model/tool budgets, task exclusions, and statistical
estimands. Every reported row is hash-bound to an observer-generated
finalization integrity record. An accepted-candidate row additionally binds its
accepted-deliverable certificate and byte-identical scoring replica; an audited
noncompletion makes neither claim. Service-alias evidence is labeled separately
from experiments using pinned model checkpoints.
